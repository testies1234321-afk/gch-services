How it works

Run npm i -g menloapp, then menloapp deploy in your public GitHub repo. You get a live link like menloapp.lol/your-app with icon, name, screenshots and description.

To try an app: menloapp try https://menloapp.lol/hello-menlo. Your Mac builds it with Xcode and your own Apple identity and delivers it to your paired iPhone.

Does this deserve to exist?

What are you trying to change?
Small, unfinished, personal ideas should reach people who will actually try them and say what to change.
What's the catch?
Trying an app needs a Mac, Xcode, an Apple Account and an iPhone. It is an early path; easier handoff is still to be built.
And the coin?
Sharing and trying apps do not require it. A Base launch via Clanker is planned and has not happened yet.
