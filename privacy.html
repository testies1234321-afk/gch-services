Privacy Policy

GCH Films Uploader. Last updated October 9, 2026.

GCH Films Uploader is a private tool used only by the GCH Services YouTube channel to upload and publish its own videos. It uses YouTube API Services.

What the tool accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the tool receives permission to manage the owner's own YouTube channel: upload videos, edit their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and privacy settings, and read the channel's own video and channel details. It doesn't access anyone else's account or any viewer data.

What it stores, and where

It stores one sign-in token so it can upload without asking the owner to sign in each time, plus a record of which videos it uploaded (their IDs and upload status). These are kept on the channel owner's own computer, in a folder only the owner can read. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's.

What it shares

Nothing. The tool doesn't sell, share or transfer any data to third parties, and it doesn't use data for advertising.

How to remove access

The owner can revoke the tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored data is deleted on request, or within 30 days after access is revoked.

Google and YouTube

This tool uses YouTube API Services. See the Google Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.
