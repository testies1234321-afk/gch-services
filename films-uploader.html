GCH Films Uploader

GCH Films Uploader is a private tool that the GCH Services YouTube channel uses to publish its own short documentary films. It isn't offered to the public and has no other users.

It uses the YouTube Data API to:

  • upload the channel's own finished films as private videos;
  • set each film's title, description, thumbnail and audience settings;
  • make a film public once the channel owner approves it, and confirm it plays.

Watch the films on GCH Services on YouTube.
