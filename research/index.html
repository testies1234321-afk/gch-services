GCH Stage 0 Research

GCH Stage 0 Research is an internal, read-only research tool operated by GCH Services for its own YouTube channel. It is not a public product, is not distributed, and has no users other than the channel owner.

What it does

Before the channel chooses topics for explainer videos, the tool reads public YouTube information — search results, video titles, durations, view counts, channel upload lists and a small sample of public top-level comments — through the YouTube Data API, to compare how much attention existing videos on a candidate topic receive. The results are used only for the channel's own editorial decisions. Nothing is published, sold or shared with third parties.

Access

The tool requests read-only YouTube access (youtube.readonly) for the channel owner's own Google account only. It cannot upload, edit or delete anything.
