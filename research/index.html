GCH Stage 0 Research

GCH Stage 0 Research is an internal, read-only research tool operated by GCH Services for its own YouTube channels. It is not a public product, is not distributed, and has no users other than the channel owner.

What it does

Before we choose and package a video, the tool reads public YouTube information through the YouTube Data API: search results, video titles, durations, publication dates, view counts and channel upload lists. It uses them to:

  • Check demand for a topic: how much attention existing videos on it receive, compared with each channel's own usual views.
  • Map comparable channels: which channels in our area have recent videos that did much better or worse than their own normal, and what those videos have in common.

The results are used only for our own editorial decisions. Nothing is published, sold or shared for anyone else's use. Comparisons such as "views relative to the channel's median" are our own calculations, not YouTube metrics.

AI relevance check

To decide whether a channel belongs to the area we research, a channel's public name and a few of its recent public video titles are sent to an AI model (Anthropic Claude) for a yes/no relevance judgment. Only the yes/no answer is kept.

Access and retention

The tool requests read-only YouTube access (youtube.readonly) for the channel owner's own Google account only. It cannot upload, edit or delete anything. YouTube API data is cached for up to 24 hours and deleted within 30 days; after that only YouTube IDs and our own calculated figures are kept. See the privacy policy for details.
