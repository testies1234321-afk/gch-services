Privacy Policy — GCH Stage 0 Research

Effective 21 October 2026.

GCH Stage 0 Research ("the tool") is an internal research tool operated by GCH Services for its own YouTube channel. It is used only by the channel owner.

What the tool accesses

  • Read-only access to YouTube (youtube.readonly) for the channel owner's own Google account, granted through Google sign-in.
  • Public YouTube data obtained through the YouTube Data API: search results, public video and channel metadata (titles, durations, publication dates, view and comment counts) and a small sample of public top-level comments.

The tool does not access any other person's private data and cannot upload, modify or delete content.

How the information is used

Only to inform the channel's own editorial decisions about which topics to research. Results stay internal. They are not published, sold, rented, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Storage and retention

Data is stored on the operator's own systems. YouTube API data is cached for up to 24 hours to avoid duplicate requests and is refreshed or deleted within 30 days. Sign-in credentials are stored with access restricted to the operator.

Cookies

The tool does not set cookies on, or collect data from, visitors to this website.

Third-party services

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account → Security → Third-party access. After revocation, stored YouTube API data is deleted within 30 days on request.
