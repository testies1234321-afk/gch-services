Privacy Policy — GCH Stage 0 Research

Effective 21 October 2026, updated 11 November 2026.

GCH Stage 0 Research ("the tool") is an internal research tool operated by GCH Services for its own YouTube channels. It is used only by the channel owner.

What the tool accesses

  • Read-only access to YouTube (youtube.readonly) for the channel owner's own Google account, granted through Google sign-in.
  • Public YouTube data obtained through the YouTube Data API: search results and public video and channel metadata (titles, durations, publication dates, view counts, subscriber counts and upload lists).

The tool does not access any other person's private data and cannot upload, modify or delete content.

How the information is used

Only to inform our own editorial decisions: which topics have demand, and which comparable channels' recent videos did unusually well or badly against their own normal. Results stay internal. They are not published, sold, rented or used for advertising. Figures such as "views relative to the channel's median" are our own calculations and are not presented as YouTube metrics.

Service providers

To judge whether a channel is relevant to our research area, a channel's public name and up to six of its recent public video titles are sent to an AI model provided by Anthropic (Claude). Only the resulting yes/no judgment is stored. No private data and no credentials are sent.

Storage and retention

Data is stored on the operator's own systems. YouTube API responses are cached for up to 24 hours to avoid duplicate requests. Stored YouTube API data (titles, counts and other metadata) is deleted within 30 days by a daily automated job. After that, only YouTube video and channel IDs and our own calculated figures (counts and ratios) are kept. Sign-in credentials are stored with access restricted to the operator.

Cookies

The tool does not set cookies on, or collect data from, visitors to this website.

Third-party services

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account → Security → Third-party access (security.google.com/settings/security/permissions). After revocation, stored YouTube API data is deleted within 30 days, or sooner on request.
